Format: talking object + twist ending, Pixar-style AI video (Weave), 30–45 s, vertical 9:16.

## The mascot

**Pip**: a small, round, bright-yellow lemon with big glossy eyes, tiny stick arms and legs, and a small green leaf on his head that droops when he's sad. He's overconfident and dramatic, and he always thinks he's the main character. Catchphrase: **"This is fine… probably."**
